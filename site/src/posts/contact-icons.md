A chat list of letters is hard to scan once half of it is people and half is machines: a friend, a coding agent, a relay, the monitoring that pages you at night. termchat.app 0.39 lets you put a small drawing in place of the letter, so you see at a glance who is who.

## Fifteen icons, drawn for termchat.app

A person, code (`</>`), a branch, a server, notes, a terminal, a bot, a pulse, a cloud, a house, a heart, a briefcase, a star, two people and a key. They are thin line drawings of our own, not a stock icon set or emoji, and the desktop and Android apps draw them from the same description in the engine. The terminal shows two characters instead, such as `<>` or `@ `.

There is no shield and no check mark on purpose: those would be easy to read as "safety number verified". An icon is decoration and says nothing about security.

## Only on your device

The icon is stored next to the name you gave the contact, and never sent anywhere:

- **the contact never learns** which icon you picked for them, or that you picked one;
- **the relay sees nothing new**: the protocol did not change;
- **your other devices keep their own**: syncing icons between your devices would be new content in the messages they exchange, and would need its own decision first.

The letter stays the default. Nothing changes until you pick an icon.

## How to pick one

- **Desktop:** **Icon** in the chat's ⋮ menu, or in the group's header.
- **Android:** a long press in the list, or the chat's ⋮ menu → **Icon**.
- **Terminal:** **Ctrl+P**, then **c**: each press moves to the next icon, and after the last one the letter comes back.

**Letter** in the picker brings the letter back.

## Settings in tabs

Settings grew long enough that finding the lock time meant scrolling past everything else. They are now in tabs: **Appearance**, **Notifications**, **Contacts and devices** and **Security** on desktop; **Appearance**, **Notifications**, **Security** and **About** on Android. A dot on the security tab says there is no backup yet. The terminal had tabs already.

The guide is in [Contacts](/docs/contacts/) and [Settings](/docs/settings/). Update from the [download page](/#install), with Homebrew, or from the notice in the app.
