termchat.app is a private text messenger. There is no account: you are a pair of keys and a nickname you chose. No phone number, no email, no contact book.

<ul>
<li><b>End-to-end encrypted</b> with the Olm double ratchet (vodozemac, the library behind Matrix). The relay sees only ciphertext and who writes to whom, and keeps undelivered messages for 14 days at most.</li>
<li><b>Add contacts by invite</b>: show a QR code or send the text. Compare the safety number to be sure.</li>
<li><b>Tor built in</b>: connect only through Tor, so the relay does not see your IP.</li>
<li><b>Disappearing messages</b>, chats without history, block and report.</li>
<li><b>A duress password</b> that erases everything when typed at unlock.</li>
<li><b>Backups</b> of your identity, and a clean way to move to another device.</li>
<li><b>No Google services.</b> Optional wake-ups through UnifiedPush and a push server of your choice; notifications never show names or text.</li>
<li>The same engine as the terminal client, and the same colour themes.</li>
</ul>

termchat.app is a free, non-commercial hobby project, licensed AGPL-3.0. It is not audited. Read the threat model before trusting it with your life: https://github.com/prophecy1345/termchat/blob/main/docs/threat-model.md
